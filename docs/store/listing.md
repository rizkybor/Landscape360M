### Description (4000)
Landscape 360 adalah aplikasi navigasi digital untuk penggiat alam terbuka: pendaki, mapala, tim SAR, peneliti lapangan, dan siapa saja yang berkegiatan jauh dari jalan raya. Explore without Limits.

LIHAT MEDAN DENGAN JELAS
• Peta 2D dan 3D dengan medan sungguhan, tinggi medan yang bisa diperbesar, dan citra satelit
• Garis kontur dengan interval pilihan (12,5 m sampai 500 m) dan grid koordinat DMS
• Koordinat DD, DMS dan UTM, serta ketinggian (mdpl) di titik mana pun
• Kompas di titik lokasi Anda yang menunjukkan arah HP
• Titik penting: pos pendakian, camp, sumber air, puncak
• Cuaca BMKG untuk kelurahan tempat Anda berada

RENCANAKAN JALUR
• Navigator: buat garis survei dengan lengkungan halus atau siku tajam, mode Straight dengan sudut tetap
• Jarak, azimuth, kemiringan dan ketinggian setiap titik
• Banyak lembar survei, tersimpan otomatis ke akun Anda dan tetap bisa dibuka tanpa sinyal
• Rute jalan kaki atau kendaraan (lewat tol atau tanpa tol) ke titik awal pendakian

REKAM PERJALANAN
• Track Recorder tetap merekam saat layar mati atau saat Anda membuka aplikasi lain
• Jarak, waktu bergerak, kecepatan, naik dan turun ketinggian
• Tersimpan di HP dulu, lalu tersinkron ke akun saat ada sinyal
• Bagikan track ke rekan satu tim atau ekspor sebagai GPX

PETA OFFLINE
• Unduh area sebelum berangkat: peta jalur dan citra satelit, sesuai batas akun Anda
• Peta, kontur dan ketinggian tetap berfungsi tanpa sinyal

TANDAI DAN BAGIKAN
• Marked Locations: sumber air, camp, bahaya, pos, dengan ikon kategori
• Marked Areas: gambar area, lihat luas dan kelilingnya, dan mode fokus yang menggelapkan sekitarnya
• Bagikan lokasi lewat WhatsApp atau aplikasi lain; penerima membukanya langsung di peta
• Ekspor laporan survei ke PNG, JPG atau PDF, lengkap dengan profil ketinggian

S.O.S
• Tahan tombol S.O.S 3 detik: lokasi, baterai dan pesan Anda terkirim ke admin setiap 30 detik, juga saat layar mati
• Tanpa sinyal, laporan menunggu di HP dan terkirim begitu sinyal kembali
• Panggil 112 atau Basarnas 115, kirim SMS berisi koordinat ke kontak darurat, sirene dan lampu S.O.S

GPS TRACKING
• Akun Pro dan Enterprise dapat membagikan posisi live ke monitor tim, juga saat layar mati

Beberapa fitur membutuhkan akun Pro atau Enterprise yang diatur oleh admin organisasi Anda. Akun dapat diajukan dari dalam aplikasi.

Catatan: penggunaan GPS di latar belakang dapat menghabiskan baterai lebih cepat. Landscape 360 tidak menggantikan perangkat darurat satelit (PLB). Selalu bawa peta, kompas dan perlengkapan yang memadai.

### App name (30)
Landscape 360

### Keywords — App Store (100)
hiking,mountain,offline map,topo,contour,gps tracker,trail,navigation,sos,survey,bearing,outdoor

### Description (4000)
Landscape 360 is a digital navigation app for people who spend their time outdoors: hikers, climbing clubs, search and rescue teams, field researchers, and anyone heading far from the road. Explore without Limits.

SEE THE TERRAIN
• 2D and 3D maps with real terrain, adjustable height and satellite imagery
• Contour lines at the interval you choose (12.5 m to 500 m) and a DMS coordinate grid
• DD, DMS and UTM coordinates and elevation anywhere on the map
• A compass at your position showing which way your phone points
• Points of interest: trail posts, camps, water sources, summits
• Indonesian weather (BMKG) for the village you are in

PLAN YOUR ROUTE
• Navigator: draw survey lines with smooth curves or sharp corners, or straight lines at fixed angles
• Distance, bearing, slope and elevation for every point
• Many survey sheets, saved to your account automatically and available without signal
• Walking or driving directions (with or without toll roads) to the trailhead

RECORD YOUR TRIPS
• The Track Recorder keeps recording with the screen off or another app open
• Distance, moving time, speed, elevation gain and loss
• Stored on your phone first, synced to your account when there is signal
• Share tracks with your team or export them as GPX

OFFLINE MAPS
• Download an area before you go: trail map and satellite imagery, within your account's limits
• The map, contours and elevation keep working without signal

MARK AND SHARE
• Marked Locations: water, camps, hazards, posts, each with its own icon
• Marked Areas: draw an area, see its size and perimeter, and focus on it with the rest dimmed
• Share a location through WhatsApp or any app; it opens straight on the map
• Export a survey report as PNG, JPG or PDF, with its elevation profile

S.O.S
• Hold the S.O.S button for 3 seconds: your location, battery and messages go to the admin every 30 seconds, also with the screen off
• Without signal, reports wait on your phone and go out as soon as signal is back
• Call 112 or Basarnas 115, text your coordinates to your emergency contacts, siren and S.O.S light

GPS TRACKING
• Pro and Enterprise accounts can share their live position with their team's monitors, also with the screen off

Some features need a Pro or Enterprise account, set up by your organisation's admin. You can request an account in the app.

Note: using GPS in the background can drain the battery faster. Landscape 360 does not replace a satellite emergency beacon (PLB). Always carry a map, a compass and proper gear.
